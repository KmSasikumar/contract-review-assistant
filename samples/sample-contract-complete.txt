MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into on 15 December 2025 (the "Effective Date") by and between Northwind Labs Private Limited, a company incorporated under the laws of India, having its registered office at 42 Residency Road, Bengaluru, Karnataka 560025 ("Northwind"), and Acme Retail Limited, a company incorporated under the laws of India, having its registered office at 18 Linking Road, Mumbai, Maharashtra 400050 ("Acme"). Northwind and Acme are each a "Party" and together the "Parties".

1. Services

Northwind will provide software development and maintenance services as described in each Statement of Work signed by both Parties.

2. Term

This Agreement starts on 1 January 2026 and continues for a term of twelve (12) months, ending on 31 December 2026 (the "Term").

3. Fees and Payment

Acme will pay Northwind INR 5,00,000 per quarter. Each invoice is payable within thirty (30) days of receipt by bank transfer to the account named on the invoice. Late payments bear interest at 1% per month.

4. Confidentiality

Each Party will keep the other Party's Confidential Information strictly confidential, will not disclose it to any third party, and will use it only to perform this Agreement. This obligation survives for three (3) years after the Agreement ends.

5. Limitation of Liability

Neither Party's total liability under this Agreement will not exceed the fees paid by Acme to Northwind in the twelve (12) months before the claim arose. Neither Party is liable for indirect, incidental or consequential damages.

6. Termination

Either Party may terminate this Agreement for convenience by giving thirty (30) days' written notice to the other Party. Either Party may terminate immediately if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within fourteen (14) days of written notice.

7. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of India. The courts at Bengaluru, Karnataka will have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute arising under this Agreement.

8. Auto-Renewal

On expiry of the Term, this Agreement will automatically renew for successive twelve (12) month periods unless either Party gives at least sixty (60) days' written notice before the end of the then-current term that it does not wish to renew.

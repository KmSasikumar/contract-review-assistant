This Consulting Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into on 20 February 2026 by and between BrightPath Consulting LLP, having its office at 7 MG Road, Pune, Maharashtra 411001 ("Consultant"), and Kite Foods Private Limited, having its office at 9 Anna Salai, Chennai, Tamil Nadu 600002 ("Client"). Consultant and Client are each a "Party" and together the "Parties".

1. Services

The Consultant will provide product strategy consulting services as described in Annex A.

2. Term

This Agreement starts on 1 March 2026 and continues for six (6) months, ending on 31 August 2026.

3. Fees and Payment

The Client will pay the Consultant INR 2,00,000 per month. Invoices are payable within fifteen (15) days of receipt by bank transfer.

Each Party agrees to keep the other Party's confidential information private and not to share it with any third party without written consent.

5. Termination

Either Party may end this Agreement by giving thirty (30) days' written notice to the other Party.

6. Intellectual Property

All deliverables created by the Consultant under this Agreement belong to the Client once the Consultant has been paid in full.

7. Entire Agreement

This Agreement is the entire agreement between the Parties on its subject matter.
